JACE CRM — Enterprise Accounting + Investor Relations + Bond Settlement Platform (Replit + GitHub)
Build an enterprise, multi-tenant CRM and real-time financial management system for an accounting, bond settlement, and investor relations company. The platform runs on Replit and integrates with Judgement Bond AI (BondAI) as the transaction + escrow agent to sync investor onboarding data, track KYC status, manage bid payment acceptance, run settlements, post real-time double-entry ledger entries, and generate settlement documents (PDF) saved to investor/customer records.
