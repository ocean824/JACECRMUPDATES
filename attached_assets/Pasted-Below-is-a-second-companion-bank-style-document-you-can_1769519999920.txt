This notice and consent applies to your investor onboarding and ongoing relationship with JACE, LLC (“JACE”) and any regulated financial, escrow, payment, settlement, identity verification, and compliance service providers used to support onboarding and transactions.

Important: Some requirements described below apply directly to regulated financial institutions (e.g., banks, broker-dealers, certain financial services providers). 2) CIP/KYC DISCLOSURE (Identity Verification & Due Diligence)
2.1 Why We Collect and Verify Identity

To help prevent money laundering, terrorist financing, fraud, and sanctions violations, JACE and/or its regulated partners may need to obtain, verify, and record information that identifies each investor and, for entity investors, their beneficial owners and control persons.

2.2 CIP NOTICE (USA PATRIOT ACT / BSA Style)

Federal law may require financial institutions to obtain, verify, and record information that identifies each person who opens an account or establishes a covered relationship. When you onboard through JACE, we may request identifying information and verification documents, and we may verify your identity through documents and/or other sources.

2.3 Information We May Collect (Examples)

Individuals (Natural Persons):

Full legal name, date of birth, residential address

Government ID (e.g., driver’s license/passport), ID number, expiration date

SSN/ITIN where applicable

Contact information (phone/email)

Entities (Legal Persons):

Legal entity name, formation jurisdiction, registration/EIN, principal address

Proof of existence (e.g., formation documents) and authority of signatory

Beneficial ownership and control person information (as applicable)

2.4 Verification Methods (Examples)

Documentary (government ID, formation documents)

Non-documentary (database checks, device/risk signals, address verification, watchlist screening)

2.5 Ongoing Monitoring & Additional Requests

You understand JACE may:

Request additional information at any time,

Delay, decline, restrict, or terminate onboarding/transactions if verification is incomplete or risk is unacceptable,

Conduct ongoing monitoring as required/appropriate for compliance and risk management.

3) PRIVACY NOTICE (Nonpublic Personal Information / NPI)
3.1 What We Mean by “Nonpublic Personal Information”

“Nonpublic personal information” generally includes information a consumer provides to obtain a financial product/service, information resulting from transactions, or information otherwise obtained about a consumer in connection with providing a financial product/service. (Concepts derived from GLBA and implementing regulations where applicable.)

3.2 Categories of Information We Collect

We may collect and store:

Identity & profile data: name, address, DOB, IDs, tax identifiers (where applicable)

Entity data: formation, ownership/control information (as applicable)

Transaction & account data: bids, settlement activity, escrow movements, payment history, invoices, ledger entries

Communications: messages, emails, support tickets, call logs (if enabled)

Technical data: IP address, device identifiers, browser/user agent, authentication logs

Compliance data: sanctions screening results, verification outcomes, risk scoring outputs

3.3 How We Use Information

We use information to:

Onboard and verify investors (CIP/KYC), fraud prevention, sanctions screening

Process transactions, escrow/settlements, invoicing, ledgering, reporting

Provide customer support, audit trails, and platform security

Meet legal/regulatory obligations and enforce policies

3.4 How We Protect Information

We maintain administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect the confidentiality and security of investor information, including role-based access controls, audit logging, encryption controls (where implemented), and least-privilege policies.

3.5 Retention

We retain records for business, legal, compliance, and audit purposes, and dispose of them in accordance with applicable retention schedules and contractual requirements with regulated partners.

4) INFORMATION SHARING: WHAT WE SHARE AND WHY
4.1 Information Sharing for Processing, Compliance, and Security

We may share information with:

Regulated escrow/payment/settlement partners (to execute transactions and compliance checks)

Identity verification & KYC vendors (to verify identity/ownership/control)

Sanctions/watchlist screening providers (OFAC-style screening support)

Cloud hosting and security providers (to store and protect data)

Auditors, counsel, and regulators/law enforcement when required or permitted by law

Service providers performing services on our behalf under contract (support, analytics, notifications)

This sharing is intended to support operational processing, compliance, fraud prevention, and platform security.
