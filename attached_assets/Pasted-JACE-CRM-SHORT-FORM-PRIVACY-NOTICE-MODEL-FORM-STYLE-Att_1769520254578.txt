FACTS — What does JACE do with your personal information?
Why?

Financial services providers choose how they share your personal information. Federal law gives consumers the right to limit some but not all sharing, and federal law requires certain disclosures about how we collect, share, and protect personal information.

What?

The types of personal information we collect and share depend on the product or service you have with us. This information can include:

Social Security number / Tax ID (where applicable) and identity information

Account, transaction, payment, settlement, and escrow activity

Contact information and communications

Device, security, and authentication data

How?

All financial services providers need to share customers’ personal information to run their everyday business. In the section below, we list the reasons financial services providers can share their customers’ personal information; the reasons JACE chooses to share; and whether you can limit this sharing.

Who we are

Who is providing this notice?
JACE, LLC (“JACE”) and its service providers and regulated escrow/payment/settlement partners supporting onboarding and transactions.

What we do
How does JACE protect my personal information?

We maintain safeguards designed to protect your personal information from unauthorized access and use, including role-based access controls, audit logging, and security monitoring.

How does JACE collect my personal information?

We collect your personal information, for example, when you:

Apply to onboard as an investor

Provide identity/ownership information (if applicable)

Initiate bids, fund escrow, or participate in settlement/payment activity

Use the portal, communicate with support, or log in from a device/browser

Why can’t I limit all sharing?

Federal law gives you the right to limit only certain sharing. Federal law also requires us to share certain information to run our everyday business and to comply with legal requirements.

Definitions

Affiliates — Companies related by common ownership or control. (JACE default: no affiliate marketing sharing.)
Nonaffiliates — Companies not related by common ownership or control. (JACE default: no sale of NPI; no nonaffiliate marketing sharing unless tenant enables it.)
Joint marketing — A formal agreement between nonaffiliated financial companies that together market financial products or services to you.
