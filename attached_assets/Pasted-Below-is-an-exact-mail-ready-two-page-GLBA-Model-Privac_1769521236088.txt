Below is an “exact, mail-ready, two-page” GLBA Model Privacy Form–style layout you can render to PDF in JACE (Print → PDF via Puppeteer/Playwright/wkhtmltopdf). It mirrors the Model Form structure (Page 1 + Page 2 components, headings, disclosure table, “To limit our sharing,” and optional mail-in opt-out form).

How to use: replace the bracketed placeholders (or bind them via your templating engine). Keep your long-form notice/consent as authoritative; this is the “model-form style” attachment.
