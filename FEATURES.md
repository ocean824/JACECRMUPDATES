# JACE CRM Features

This document outlines the full feature set of the JACE CRM, including both the original functionality and the new modules added.

## Core CRM & Investor Management

- **Investor Tracking**: Manage investors from lead to closed deal through a structured pipeline (Lead → Contacted → KYC → Documents → Ready → Closed).
- **KYC Workflow**: A dedicated module to handle Know Your Customer (KYC) processes, including document uploads and status tracking.
- **Document Management**: Centralized repository for all investor and compliance-related documents.
- **Communications Hub**: Log and track all communications with investors, including emails, calls, and meetings.
- **Task Management**: Create, assign, and track tasks. Includes a recurring task feature for routine activities.
- **Calendar & Email**: Integrated calendar for scheduling and a basic email client for communication.

## Accounting & Financials

- **Chart of Accounts**: Fully customizable chart of accounts to categorize all financial transactions.
- **Double-Entry Journal**: A robust journal entry system that enforces double-entry bookkeeping principles.
- **Bond Settlements**: Track the issuance and settlement of bonds.
- **Financial Reporting**: Generate key financial statements:
    - **Trial Balance**: Verify that total debits equal total credits.
    - **Income Statement**: View revenues, expenses, and net income for a selected period.
    - **Balance Sheet**: Get a snapshot of assets, liabilities, and equity.

## New! Invoicing & Expenses

- **Invoicing Module**: Create, send, and track professional invoices. 
    - Convert accepted quotes directly into invoices.
    - Track invoice status (Draft, Sent, Paid, Overdue).
- **Quotes**: Generate and send quotes to potential customers.
- **Expense Tracking**: Record and manage all business expenses. 
    - Categorize expenses for better analysis.
    - Workflow for expense approval.
- **Escrow Accounts**: Manage funds held in escrow, with transaction logging for deposits and withdrawals.

## New! Human Resources (HR)

- **Employee Management**: Maintain a central database of all employees with their contact information, roles, and salary details.
- **Department Management**: Organize your company structure by creating and managing departments.
- **Time Tracking**: Log and monitor employee work hours against projects.
- **Leave Requests**: A complete system for employees to request time off and for managers to approve or reject requests.
- **Payroll Management**: Process payroll, calculate net pay, and track payment status.

## New! BondAI Integration

- **Payment Processing**: Seamlessly connect with BondAI to process payments from investors.
- **Webhook Monitoring**: A dedicated dashboard to monitor incoming webhook events from BondAI, ensuring reliable data synchronization.
- **Health Dashboard**: Real-time status of the BondAI integration, including webhook configuration and event success/failure rates.

## System & Administration

- **Analytics Dashboard**: High-level overview of key CRM metrics.
- **Audit Logs**: Comprehensive logging of all actions taken within the system for compliance and security.
- **Custom Fields**: Extend the CRM by adding custom fields to various modules.
- **Data Import**: Bulk import data into the CRM.
- **User & Admin Roles**: Separate login and access control for regular users and administrators.
- **Investor Portal**: A dedicated portal for investors to view their information (feature stubbed).
